How APOD Email Works

Every day, APOD Email fetches NASA's latest Astronomy Picture of the Day and sends it straight to your inbox—no tracking pixels, no subscription fees, and no clutter.

What's in Each Email

NASA's daily APOD image (or direct video link), the full official astronomer-written explanation, and photographer and NASA credits.

Daily Delivery

Sent once per day in the late morning, US Central Time, shortly after the official APOD entry is published.

Free & Independent

Independently operated since 2009 and open-source on GitHub. Always free, with one-click unsubscribe in every email.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is APOD Email?

APOD Email is a free, independent daily email service operating since 2009 that delivers NASA's Astronomy Picture of the Day directly to your inbox.

Is APOD Email free?

Yes. APOD Email is completely free to subscribe to and use, with no paid tiers or subscription fees. I host and pay for this service myself so donations are always welcome!

What is included in each daily email and when is it sent?

Each email includes NASA's daily Astronomy Picture of the Day image or video link, the complete official explanation written by a professional astronomer, and photographer and NASA credits. Emails are sent once per day in the late morning, US Central Time.

Is APOD Email affiliated with NASA?

No. APOD Email is an independent, open-source project maintained by Greg Tracy and is not officially affiliated with or endorsed by NASA, Michigan Technological University, or the official APOD editors.

How do I unsubscribe and how is my email used?

Your email address is used solely to deliver the daily APOD email and is never sold or shared with third parties. You can unsubscribe at any time with one click via the link in every email or using the unsubscribe form on this page.
